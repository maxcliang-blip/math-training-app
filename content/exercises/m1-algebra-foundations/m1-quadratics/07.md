solutionLatex: |-
  By the Pythagorean theorem: $x^2 + (x+1)^2 = (x+2)^2$.
  $$x^2 + x^2 + 2x + 1 = x^2 + 4x + 4$$
  $$x^2 - 2x - 3 = 0 \implies (x-3)(x+1) = 0$$
  $x = 3$ or $x = -1$; lengths rule out $-1$, so $x = 3$.
