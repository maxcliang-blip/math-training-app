solutionLatex: |-
  The domain excludes $x = 0$ and $x = -3$.
  Multiply by $2x(x+3)$:
  $$2(x+3) + 2x = x(x+3)$$
  $$4x + 6 = x^2 + 3x$$
  $$x^2 - x - 6 = (x-3)(x+2) = 0$$
  Both candidates are in the domain, so the sum is $3 + (-2) = 1$.
